# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static, hand-written HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript deployed through GitHub Pages at `innerreset.life`. There is no framework, build step, or bundler.

## Users

People who want a private place to express something difficult, unfinished, or important and receive a personal artifact they can keep and revisit.

## Product Purpose

InnerReset turns spoken or written personal expression into a finished, private audio artifact. The core model is Express → Transform → Receive → Keep. Success means a person can give the product something real and receive something meaningful that belongs to them.

## Positioning

InnerReset is a private ritual space for thought, voice, direction, and emotional processing. It creates finite personal artifacts rather than endless conversations, public posts, or gamified self-improvement signals.

## Operating Context

The product has one Create entry point and two flagship experiences: Words to Carry for the moment a person is in, and A Mind to Build for a direction they want to strengthen. Carries and Mind Tracks live in separate private libraries.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- People may speak or type.
- Words to Carry returns one finished voiced Carry.
- A Mind to Build starts with an intention, preserves a user review and approval step for the exact words, offers Clear, Quiet, and Subliminal presentation modes, and produces a Mind Track from the approved wording.
- Optional practice comes after value; progress is qualitative and user-controlled.
- The product is not an AI companion, endless chat, social network, task manager, fake call, or streak- or score-based habit app.
- The public app is not yet in an app store. The launch action is early access by email.
- The marketing site must make no medical, clinical, therapist-equivalence, encryption, or compliance claims.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is InnerReset. The canonical mark at `assets/brand/innerreset-mark.png` must only be scaled or glowed, never redrawn, recolored, distorted, or substituted. Language is concise, human, controlled, and emotionally literate.

## Evidence on Hand

- Thirteen real product screenshots at `assets/screens/`.
- The canonical brand mark at `assets/brand/innerreset-mark.png`.
- No permissioned testimonials, app-store links, or active social accounts are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Give the product something real; receive something worth keeping.
- Personal artifacts, not public performance.
- Finished creations, not endless interaction.
- User approval where wording matters.
- Progress remains qualitative and under the user’s control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site requires semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient text contrast, meaningful screenshot alternatives, touch-friendly targets, no autoplay audio, and full reduced-motion support.
