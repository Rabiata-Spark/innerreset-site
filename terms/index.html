12. Sound layers and listening modes

12.1 Sound layers and listening modes are listening choices. No health effect is intended or promised.

12.2 Tone layers you can add:

  • a steady tone at a pitch you choose (for example 432 Hz or 528 Hz);
  • a binaural-style layer: two slightly different tones, one in each ear (headphones recommended);
  • an isochronic layer: a tone with a gentle, regular pulse.

12.3 Listening modes for Mind Tracks. "Clear" puts your words in front, "Quiet" sets them lower, and "Subliminal" plays your approved words low in the mix. There are no hidden messages: nothing is added or changed, and you hear exactly the lines you approved, only quieter. The app asks you to confirm before using this mode.

12.4 Listening safety. Keep the volume moderate. Do not listen while driving or when you need to stay alert. Stop if you feel discomfort. If you have epilepsy or are sensitive to pulsing sounds, ask a doctor before using pulsed layers.
