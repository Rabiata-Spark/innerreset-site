1. The short version

What you share with InnerReset exists to create your personal audio, and for nothing else. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising, and other users never see your content. When you speak, the transcription happens through Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone and only the text reaches us; we do not store your voice recordings. Your words are processed by a small number of AI and audio providers under contracts that limit them to providing the service, and we use API tiers whose terms do not permit training on your content. You can export everything and delete everything from Settings, and anything you delete stays recoverable for 14 days before it is gone for good.

2. Who we are

Rabiata LLC operates InnerReset and is the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at support@innerreset.life for any question or request about your data. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the rights described in section 9 apply to you and you can also contact your local data-protection authority.

4. The legal grounds we rely on

  • Performing our contract with you: creating your artifacts, keeping your library, running your subscription and credits, answering support requests.
  • Your consent: using the microphone and speech recognition, and the AI-processing acknowledgement you give before the first creation. You can withdraw consent at any time in iOS Settings or by writing to us; the related feature then stops, and what was created before stays yours.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and reliable, preventing abuse, and improving the product with pseudonymous analytics that never include your content. You may object (section 9).
  • Legal obligations: tax and accounting records, and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

5. AI processing

InnerReset writes and voices your artifacts with the help of third-party providers acting as our processors:

  • Language models (Anthropic and OpenAI) read the words you shared, ask at most one clarifying question, write the script of a Carry or the lines of a Mind Track, and evaluate that the result stays true to what you said.
  • Voice synthesis (ElevenLabs) turns the approved words into speech in the voice you selected.

We send these providers only the text needed for the task, never your name, email address or identifiers. Each provider is bound by a data-processing agreement, and we use API tiers whose terms do not permit them to train models on your content. An automated safety classification runs on what you share, only to route unsafe requests to protective or crisis content; it makes no medical determinations and no decision with legal effects on you. Nothing you make is shown to other users, and no artifact is ever rewritten without your action.

6. Where your data lives and who helps us run the service

Our servers and database run on Railway, generated audio and images are stored on Cloudflare R2, and short-lived job queues run on Redis. These are located mainly in the United States. Apple provides sign-in with Apple, speech recognition, the App Store and TestFlight; Google provides sign-in with Google if you choose it; RevenueCat processes subscription status for us; PostHog hosts our pseudonymous product analytics; Sentry receives crash and error reports; an email provider delivers one-time sign-in codes. Each acts under a contract that restricts them to providing their service to us.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, your data is transferred to the United States. We rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum) with each provider, and on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified under it.

8. Your controls in the app

  • Export: Settings → Privacy & data → Prepare data export gives you a complete machine-readable copy of your account, expressions, artifacts, Journey and consents.
  • Delete a Carry or a Mind Track: swipe, or use the menu of the artifact. An Undo line stays for a few seconds, and the artifact remains recoverable for 14 days.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account. You can cancel within 14 days by signing in again.
  • Microphone and speech: allow or revoke at any time in iOS Settings → InnerReset. You can always type instead of speaking.
  • Downloads: Settings → Remove downloaded audio deletes the local copies on your iPhone.
  • Subscription: managed entirely in your Apple ID settings (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions) or from the plan sheet in the app.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to receive it in a portable format, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. Write to support@innerreset.life and we will answer within one month, or tell you if we need longer for a complex request. We may ask you to confirm the request from the email address of your account. You can also complain to a supervisory authority; in France that is the CNIL (cnil.fr), and in the United Kingdom the ICO (ico.org.uk).

If you live in California, the CCPA/CPRA gives you the rights to know, delete and correct your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months. You may use an authorized agent; we will verify the request through your account email.

10. Sharing and disclosure

We share personal data only with the AI providers named in section 5 and the service providers listed in section 6, strictly so they can provide their service to us. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never show it to other users. We disclose data when the law compels us to, and we would tell you unless we are prohibited from doing so. If InnerReset were ever acquired or merged, your data would remain covered by this policy and you would be told before any change.

11. Security

Encryption in transit everywhere, scoped credentials for each provider, server-side authorization on every request, private storage objects that are never publicly addressable, and deletion pipelines that remove storage objects rather than just database rows. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affected you, we would notify you and the competent authorities as the law requires.

12. Children

InnerReset is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use it. We do not knowingly process data of anyone younger, and we delete accounts we find to belong to minors.

13. The website

innerreset.life is a static site served by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no third-party fonts or scripts, and has no forms; the only way to contact us from it is by email. GitHub may keep standard server logs for security under its own privacy statement.
