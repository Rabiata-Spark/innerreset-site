1. The short version

What you share with InnerReset exists to create your personal audio, and for nothing else. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising, and other users never see your content. Your raw voice recordings are deleted right after processing unless you choose to keep your voice in the final audio. You can export everything or delete everything, in Settings, at any time.

3. AI processing

To create artifacts, your expressions are processed by specialized third-party AI providers acting as our processors — large-language-model and voice/audio providers operating under contracts that restrict use. We use API tiers whose terms do not permit training on your content. Automated safety classification is applied to what you share, only to route unsafe requests to protective or crisis content; it makes no medical determinations.

4. Where data lives

Application data and generated audio are stored with our hosting and storage providers, with encryption in transit. Data may be processed in countries other than yours, with appropriate safeguards.

5. Your controls

  • Export: Settings → export gives you a complete machine-readable copy (account, expressions, artifacts, consents).
  • Deletion: Settings → delete account removes your account and content after a short grace period, including generated audio files in storage. Artifact deletion inside the app also purges the underlying audio files.
  • Consents: microphone, AI processing, and quiet-layer transparency consents are recorded and revocable; revoking stops the related feature.
  • Rights (where applicable law grants them): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, and complaint to your supervisory authority. Write to support@innerreset.life.

6. Sharing

Only with the processors described above, strictly to operate the service — never sold, never shared for advertising, never public. We disclose data if legally compelled, and we would tell you unless prohibited.

7. Security

Transport encryption, scoped credentials, server-side authorization on every request, and deletion pipelines that remove storage objects — not just database rows. No system is perfectly secure; we notify affected users of breaches as the law requires.

8. Children

InnerReset is not directed at children and requires a minimum age of 18. We do not knowingly process data of anyone younger; accounts found to belong to minors are deleted.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and notify you in the app of material ones before they take effect.
