3. Words we use

  • Creation: a Carry (a short spoken message made from what you share) or a Mind Track (a short self-talk track whose lines you approve before they are spoken). A creation includes its text, title, audio, optional cover art, and the settings you chose.
  • Expression: what you type, the text of what you say, and any answer you give to a clarifying question, when you ask for a creation.
  • Guest: a person who uses the app without signing in. The app can make one free creation for a guest.
  • Account: the record we create when you sign in with Apple. It keeps your library, plan and credits across devices.
  • Provider: a company that processes personal data for us under a contract (a processor).

7. How a creation is made, and which AI providers take part

Each creation passes through a few steps. Our server runs them; the providers below do the model work.

  1. Reading and safety. A language model reads your expression, checks it for safety (section 8), and forms a reading of what you want to say. It may ask you one clarifying question.
  2. Writing and checking. A language model writes the script of a Carry or the lines of a Mind Track, then checks that the result stays true to what you said. You approve the exact lines of a Mind Track before they are spoken.
  3. Voicing. A speech-synthesis model turns the approved text into speech in the voice you chose.
  4. Cover art (optional). If you ask for cover art, an image model makes a picture from a short description that our language model writes from your creation.

8. The automated safety check

What it does. Before anything is written, a language model classifies your expression into one of four levels, from S0 (no concern) to S3 (signs of immediate risk to you or someone else). It also records the topic of any concern. Our server applies fixed rules to the result:

  • S0 and S1: the creation is made normally.
  • S2 (signs of distress that are not immediate): the creation is made, written in a more protective way.
  • S3 (signs of immediate risk): the creation is stopped. No audio is made. The app shows crisis resources for your language and region instead.

What happens to the data when a creation is stopped.

  • The text of the stopped expression is deleted from our database by our regular maintenance job, normally within about 5 minutes.
  • We keep a short safety record: the level, the topic, the route applied, the version of the check, and the time. It contains none of your words.
  • The credits held for the creation are returned to you.
  • The provider that ran the check may keep the text for up to 30 days to detect abuse (section 7).

This is an automated decision. No person reviews it before it applies. It has no legal effect on you. It can affect you, because it stops a creation you asked for, and a check of this kind can be wrong. We use it because we cannot safely make audio from words that suggest someone is in immediate danger. The legal condition for it is your explicit consent; section 5 explains that the step that records it is still being added.

Your right to human review. If you think a creation was stopped by mistake, write to support@innerreset.life. A person will review the decision, taking account of what you tell us, and you can explain your point of view and contest the decision. Because the stopped text is deleted, we may ask you to send it again or describe it. We will tell you what we found. If the decision was wrong, we will help you make the creation, and we use what we learn to improve the check. Your credits were already returned.

InnerReset is not a crisis service. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

9. Aggregate insights about how InnerReset is used

To understand how people use InnerReset and to improve it, we keep a short summary with each Carry version. It contains:

  • the type of Carry and its point of view;
  • the kind of relationship involved, if any;
  • a short description, written by our language model, of the support you asked for and of the emotional tone;
  • the safety level and topic;
  • the form of the text and the language.

It never contains your expression, the script, the lines or the title.

This summary is pseudonymous, not anonymous. It is stored with your creation, so it stays linked to your account or guest identity. It is never shown in the app and never used to make decisions about you.

How we use it. We use it only to produce aggregate statistics across many creations. Our reports count creations and never show a row per person. Any relationship, tone or safety category that appears in fewer than five creations in a report is merged into "other" and its label is not shown. We never try to identify a person from a report.

How long we keep it. It is deleted with the Carry, when the Carry is permanently deleted or when your account is deleted.

Your choice. This use needs your explicit consent and relies on our legitimate interests. Today the summary is kept for every Carry, and the app does not yet ask for this consent separately; we are adding an optional setting for it. You can withdraw or object at any time by writing to us (sections 5 and 17), without affecting the rest of the service.

10. Speech and voice

When you hold the Speak control, your iPhone captures audio from the microphone and passes it to Apple's speech recognition. Depending on your iPhone and its settings, Apple processes it on the device or on Apple's servers, under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. InnerReset receives only the resulting text. We never receive, record or store your voice. You can always type instead of speaking.

You can allow or revoke microphone and speech-recognition access at any time in iOS Settings → InnerReset.

The text of what you say is treated like any other expression (sections 5 and 14).

  • If you believe someone has shared information about you through InnerReset, write to us. We will help as far as we can without revealing the other person's data.

20. Security

  • All data travels encrypted between the app, our servers and our providers.
  • Our server checks your permission on every request. Your creations are visible only to you.
  • Audio and images are stored privately. They are reached only through links that expire, and are never publicly listed.
  • Each provider has its own restricted credentials.
  • Our logs remove the content of expressions, scripts and lines before anything is written.
  • Your sign-in and guest tokens are stored in your iPhone's Keychain, on that device only. They are not synchronised to other devices.
  • When you delete something, our deletion jobs remove the stored files, not only the database records.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify the competent authorities and you as the law requires. Under the GDPR, we notify the authority within 72 hours where required.
