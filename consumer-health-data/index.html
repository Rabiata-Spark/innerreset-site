Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Applies to: the InnerReset app for iPhone and the website innerreset.life

1. About this notice

This notice is for people whose consumer health data we collect in Washington (My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373), Nevada (SB 370, NRS Chapter 603A) or Connecticut (Connecticut Data Privacy Act). It applies if you live in one of these states, or if your data is collected there. It adds to our Privacy Policy, which explains our practices in full.

"Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status. It includes information that we derive or infer from other information.

A creation is a Carry or a Mind Track made for you in the app. An expression is what you type or say, and any answer you give to a clarifying question, when you ask for a creation.

2. Consumer health data we collect, and why

InnerReset makes personal audio from what you share about your inner life. Because of that, we treat the following as consumer health data:

Category Why we collect it
Your expressions: what you type, the text of what you say, and your answers to a clarifying question. They may reveal your emotional state, your mental health or your relationships To make, store and play the creation you asked for
Short answers you give to reflection questions in your Journey To show you your own Journey
The reading our language model makes of your expression: the kind of support you ask for, the relationship involved, the emotional tone To write a creation that fits what you said
The result of our automated safety check: a level from S0 to S3, the topic of any concern, and the route applied To show crisis resources and stop a creation when your words suggest immediate risk, and to write more protectively when they suggest distress
A pseudonymous summary of the reading and the safety result, kept with each Carry To produce aggregate statistics about how InnerReset is used. Reports never show an individual, and any category that appears fewer than five times is merged into "other"
Creations made from your expressions: scripts, lines, titles, audio and optional cover art To provide your library

We do not collect precise location, biometric data, genetic data, or data from health apps or devices. We never receive or store your voice: Apple's speech recognition turns it into text on your iPhone or on Apple's servers, under Apple's terms, and we receive only the text.

3. Sources

We collect consumer health data from two sources:

  • you, when you type, speak or answer a question in the app;
  • our own systems, which derive the reading, the safety result and the summary from your expressions, using the language-model providers listed in section 4.

We do not buy consumer health data or receive it from data brokers.

4. Who receives your consumer health data

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with third parties for their own purposes, and we do not use it for advertising.

We disclose it only to processors: companies that process it on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us.

Processor What it receives Purpose
OpenAI The text of your expression and the text generated from it; for optional cover art, a short image description written from your creation Reading, safety check, writing, checking; cover art if you ask for it
Anthropic The same text as OpenAI Backup language model. Not switched on today
ElevenLabs The approved text to be spoken Speech synthesis
Railway All data held by the service Servers, database and job queue
Cloudflare Audio files and cover images Storage
Our email service Messages you send to support, if you include health information Support mailbox

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data. We may disclose consumer health data when a law, court order or other legal process requires it, and we will tell you unless the law forbids it.

No third party collects consumer health data through our app over time and across other apps or websites. The app runs no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

5. Your consent

We collect and use your expressions, the reading and the safety result because they are necessary to make the creation you ask for. Accepting our Terms is not consent.

What is missing today. The app does not yet ask for consent in a separate step. That matters in two places, and we are adding a consent screen and an optional setting to fix both:

  • the pseudonymous summary used for aggregate statistics (section 2) is not necessary to provide the service, so it needs your consent; today it is kept for every Carry;
  • Connecticut law requires consent to process sensitive data, even for the service itself.

Until then, you can ask us at any time to delete your summaries or anything you shared.

We do not share consumer health data with third parties. If we ever wished to, we would first ask for your separate consent, which you could refuse.

Withdrawing consent. The app has no control for this yet: write to support@innerreset.life. When you withdraw consent:

  • for the summary: we delete the summaries linked to you, and keep deleting new ones until the setting exists; the service keeps working;
  • for all processing: we stop making new creations for you, because the service cannot work without reading what you share. Your library stays yours to export, play or delete.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data;
  • access it, and receive a list of all third parties and affiliates with whom we have shared or sold it, with their contact details;
  • withdraw your consent to its collection and sharing;
  • delete it. When you ask, we delete it from our records, including our backups, and we tell each processor to delete it.

We will not treat you differently, charge you a different price or give you a lower quality of service for using these rights.

How to use your rights. In the app, you can export your data (Settings → Privacy & data → Prepare data export), delete a creation, or delete your account. For any other request, email support@innerreset.life with the subject "Consumer health data request", or, once we publish a telephone number here, call us (until then, email is the fastest way to reach us). We may ask you to confirm the request from the app or from the email address linked to your account, so that no one else can obtain or delete your data. If you used InnerReset without an account, we can find your data only with information from the device you used. You may use an authorised agent; we will check the agent's authority.

When we answer. Within 45 days of receiving your request. If we need more time, we may extend this once by 45 more days where reasonably necessary, and we will tell you why within the first 45 days. Requests are free, up to twice a year.

Appeal. If we refuse your request, in full or in part, we will tell you why. You can appeal by replying to our answer with the word "Appeal", within a reasonable time. We will answer your appeal in writing within 45 days in Washington and Nevada, and within 60 days in Connecticut, and explain our decision. If you disagree with the result, you can contact:

  • Washington: the Office of the Attorney General, atg.wa.gov;
  • Nevada: the Office of the Attorney General, ag.nv.gov;
  • Connecticut: the Office of the Attorney General, portal.ct.gov/ag.

7. Geofencing and sale

We do not use geofencing. We do not collect precise location. We do not sell consumer health data, and we have never done so.

8. How long we keep it

We keep consumer health data only as long as we need it for the purposes in section 2. The Privacy Policy, section 14, gives each period. In short: creations stay until you delete them or your account; your original words stay until you delete your account; the text of an expression stopped by the safety check is deleted within minutes; account deletion takes effect at once.

9. Changes to this notice

If we make a material change to this notice, we will post it here with a new effective date and tell you in the app before it takes effect. If a change means collecting or sharing consumer health data in a new way, we will ask for your consent first. You can review this notice at any time on our website and in the app, and ask us to explain any part of it.
