A little clarity before you begin.

What’s the difference between Carry and Mind?

Words to Carry starts with the moment you’re in and creates a personal voiced reflection. A Mind to Build starts with an intention: you review and approve the exact wording that becomes your Mind Track.

Do I have to record my voice?

No. You can speak or type. Begin in whichever way feels easier.

Who is InnerReset for?

People who want a private place for expression and intentional self-talk. The male and female illustrations are part of the same visual world; neither experience is limited to a gender.

Is this therapy or an AI companion?

No. InnerReset creates personal audio, not an ongoing companion relationship. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care.

Is my content public?

There is no social feed. Your Carries and Mind Tracks live in your personal libraries. For details about processing, service providers, storage, and deletion, read our privacy policy.

How can I try it?

InnerReset is an iPhone app in early access. Email us to request access and hear about availability. There is no public App Store download link here yet.
